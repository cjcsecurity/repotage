# repotage self-demo: storylines

The one thing: point repotage at a repo and you get a real, narrated demo film, made locally.
Spine: this film's own material. The voice you hear, its prompt, its takes and scores, the music bed and the timeline all appear on screen as themselves.

## A. The film explains itself (picked)
Device: the object is the explanation. Form: one frame that rebuilds.
Frame 0 is this film's own timeline: the narrator's waveform drawing under the one-sentence prompt that designed the voice. Each lane (music, captions, cuts) arrives as it is named, then the timeline folds into the repo. Every later beat shows the real artifact of that stage: the script file, the capture of the review page, the takes and their scores, the ducked bed, the build and check output, the doctor.
- Beats: hook (the voice reveals its own prompt) → reveal (name + command) → script → capture → voice → music → compose → runs locally → end card.
- Look: warm graphite #121110, bone #f3efe6, forge orange #ff6a1a. Inter 800 for the wordmark, JetBrains Mono for data.
- Sound: bright 100 BPM synth bed, its lift on the name; clicks on real clicks; a pop on the end card.

## B. Before and after
A person spends a day in a screen recorder and an editor, then the same film arrives from one command.

## C. Recursion as a gag
The film keeps zooming into its own timeline, which contains the film, which contains the timeline.

Recommendation: A, because it proves the claim with the film's own artifacts instead of describing them.
